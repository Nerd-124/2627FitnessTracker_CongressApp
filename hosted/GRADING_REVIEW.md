# Grading review status

PROTOTYPE — independent health, mental-health and accessibility review pending.
The official WHO-5 instrument and score must not be described as validation of
this app's A–E mapping or combined Exercise/Mental health/Sleep snapshot.

Exercise/sleep references, source provenance, missing-data handling and current-
age safeguards are retained. WHO-5 items, answer options, two-week recall and
raw-total ×4 scoring are reproduced unchanged with attribution and a source link.
The current complete assessment is used for 14 days, not silently imputed from
stress, energy, pulse or old answers. Skipping the questionnaire is allowed.

App letters use the existing reference-ratio bands: A >=1; B >=.75; C >=.5;
D >0 below .5; E exactly 0. WHO-5 supplies score/100 for this display. Overall is
the mean of known primary ratios capped at 1, requiring at least two of three.
Neither letters nor the combined result are WHO diagnostic categories. They do
not measure moral worth, mental illness, clinical risk, biological age or lifespan.
Higher scores cannot rule out distress; lower scores lead to supportive language.
BPM remains hidden report context without an unvalidated score contribution.

Review questions before a formal health claim or launch:
- Does a letter display for well-being create stigma or conceal uncertainty?
- Should the combined result be replaced with three separate comparisons?
- Is the 14-day freshness display clear alongside weekly exercise/sleep data?
- Are support resources, optional participation and private-account storage clear?
- Is the interface usable for students, disabilities and varying cultural contexts?
- Does the app clearly distinguish the standardized WHO-5 score from its custom UI?
