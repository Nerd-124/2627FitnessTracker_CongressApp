California demo route attribution

Three continuous extracts of the recorded 2016 Santa Rosa Marathon track distributed with leaflet-gpx. Original coordinates, elevations and timing are retained. They are read-only demonstrations, not a user’s activities. Source revision: 90582f1543552cda13335dfe3534c8b5e4caf108. Calorie estimates use illustrative body mass; temperatures are requested from Open-Meteo’s historical model, not present in the source recording.
