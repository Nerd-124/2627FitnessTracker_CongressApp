Import your recording

Export a CSV from your EEG device, then match the format below. This is a file viewer; it does not connect to a device or detect sleep stages.

Band-power formatRaw EEG format

CSV: time_s,amplitude_uv (microvolts), or time_s,delta,theta,alpha,beta,gamma (device band power). 2–5,000 samples; at most 1 MB. Time must increase; use consistent units. Up to 10 recordings.

Curious how it works?

Try a clearly labeled sample to learn the controls. The sample is computer-generated and is never added to your health records.

Delta, theta, alpha, beta, and gamma are frequency bands. A band’s value alone does not establish a sleep stage or sleep quality.

CURIOSITY, WITH CONTEXT

Behind the science.

Understand the model before the number.

Longevity Simulator is an educational prototype. It does not estimate your lifespan, biological age, or probability of disease. Its future curve is a deliberately illustrative visualization of habits, not a validated longitudinal research model.

How the habit index works

Movement contributes 40%, sleep 35%, and fruit and vegetable servings 25%. Movement reaches its cap at 150 minutes per week, servings at 5 per day, and sleep peaks at 8 hours. These weights and the exponential curve are design choices, not clinical findings. Both curves begin at an arbitrary index of 65; the reference follows your saved starting habits. Neither is a population average. A high score does not guarantee good health.

Resting energy and BMI

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)². Resting energy uses Mifflin–St Jeor: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age, with −161 or +5 as the published sex-based coefficients. Showing both avoids inferring a coefficient. The estimate is not a food intake prescription. These adult equations do not assess children.

Elevation-aware activity energy

ACSM-style oxygen cost: walking = 0.1 × speed + 1.8 × speed × grade + 3.5; running = 0.2 × speed + 0.9 × speed × grade + 3.5, with speed in m/min. kcal ≈ oxygen cost × kg / 1000 × 5 × minutes. Loaded routes are evaluated by segment with constant-speed allocation; negative slopes are treated as level ground. Manual mode uses total ascent. Missing elevations contribute no ascent, so review coverage and GPS noise. Broad ±25% bounds are illustrative, not a statistical confidence interval. Walking is most appropriate around 3–6 km/h; running at 8 km/h or faster. Temperature and humidity contribute to a NOAA-style heat-index calculation where applicable, with no invented calorie correction. Heat index assumes shade and light wind, and does not establish safe exercise conditions.

AI and device connections

The manual planner uses transparent rules. The free conversational fitness coach uses built-in rules in your browser. It recognizes a limited set of goals and preferences and lets you choose a plan before saving anything. It is not an AI model or a medical prescription. Heart rate, blood pressure, glucose, sleep, and EEG notes are entered manually. Brainwaves require compatible EEG hardware and cannot be measured by a phone alone. EEG CSV and GPX imports work without a device connection. Street maps use Leaflet and OpenStreetMap on request; current weather comes from Open-Meteo. Live wearable integration, a validated risk model, remain future work.

Your data belongs to you

Records, imported EEG, and saved routes remain in this browser's local storage; calculations run in Python inside your browser. The Python runtime loads from jsDelivr; ordinary simulation inputs stay in your browser. The free chat coach also runs in your browser. Its conversation and selected routine context are not sent to an AI provider. Conversations stay in this tab; only goals and plans you choose are saved locally. ChatGPT provides sign-in. Records are separated by signed-in account and stored locally on this browser; there is no health-record cloud synchronization or advertising. Browser storage is not encrypted: use a private device and demo data for presentations. Export or delete your data at any time. Loading the street map shares map-area requests with OpenStreetMap and downloads Leaflet from jsDelivr. Fetching weather sends the last route coordinate to Open-Meteo. Opening external research links shares a request with that website.
