Your website, as an iPhone app.

This Xcode project opens the live website, so published website changes appear in the app after a fresh launch or reload.

Download Xcode project ZIP ↓

  1. Download and extract the ZIP in Finder.
  2. Open LongevityLive.xcodeproj inside the extracted folder.
  3. Choose an iPhone simulator in Xcode and press Command-R.
  4. Tap Continue with Google and choose your Google account.
